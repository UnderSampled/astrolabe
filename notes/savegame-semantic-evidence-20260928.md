The engine normalizes registration flags in `004c66c0:18-23`; the player writer selects `0x0300` and its state-3 load applies mask 4 (`00402780:56-65`, `00431760:15-23`). These are distinct guarded paths, not two unconditional applies on every load.

The state-6 transition in `00404700:28-36` calls `004312c0` to save the **outgoing** named level before it copies the pending level name and enters state 5. The call continues through `004314f0` → `004c6490` → `004c6d60(..., 0x0c00)`. The target actor NeverBack latch `1:5:833347` has flags `0x0a0c` and is eligible. This order invalidates the initial assumption that an opposed `manoir.sav` placed before first-slot startup would remain opposed after leaving manor.
