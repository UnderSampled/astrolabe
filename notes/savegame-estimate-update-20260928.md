04:17 UTC to the pause checkpoint around 14:04 UTC. The supplied save has
1,271 distinct contextual identities: 1,222 owned/typed (about 96%), 508
complete meanings (about 40%), and 763 unknown meanings, including 398 with

Thirty-seven levels have structural descriptor discovery, not complete semantic

The complete-meaning count remained 508 from the 10:26 review through the pause.

The plateau nevertheless rules out extrapolating the early bulk-family rate
to the remaining records. Unsupported AI operations, unresolved native callback
edges, dialogue/map content links and unvisited progression states dominate the
remaining uncertainty. The final dialogue probe also exposed a bounded Orrery

These are judgment-based planning ranges for the observed Sol-led workflow with
bounded Luna delegation and small useful implementation improvements. They are
not measured completion-time predictions or sums of every worker's hours. The
ranges are not additive. At eight active hours per day they represent about
5–10 working days for the supplied save and 2.5–5+ working weeks for the broader
goal. Continuous unattended calendar time and account usage limits are separate.

Confidence is low, especially for the broader goal. The remaining fields are
harder than the early families; approximately 40% of records explained does
not imply 40% of effort spent. A verified shared callback could explain many
fields at once, while inaccessible states, incomplete source coverage or another
instrumentation gap could push the long tail beyond these ranges. More Luna
agents alone do not remove those dependencies, and no controlled comparison
establishes a delegation speedup or token saving.

On an eventual resumption, use the first 4–6 active hours as a reassessment
window: fix the identified observer failure, pursue a concrete missing semantic
bridge, and measure new verified meanings and resolved shared blockers. Do not
spend that window repeating exhausted structural surveys. Recalibrate from the
result rather than silently extending the old estimate or treating another
ownership/index milestone as completion. This is a review point, not an
automatic stopping rule or permission to resume now.

These estimates concern save research. Comprehensive Godot gameplay and AI API
parity remain a separate, larger effort; this investigation has not measured
enough representative implementation work to give that whole port a credible
completion estimate. Existing useful persistence behavior and bounded port/tool
improvements remain documented separately from semantic coverage.
